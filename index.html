Our Mission · Why We Do This

“Don’t create another trip. Fill the empty one.”

India doesn't need more empty trucks burning expensive diesel on the roads. When an empty truck drives back from a delivery, why should a business owner or farmer pay full price to hire another vehicle? LoadLink fills that empty return space — saving customers up to 35%, putting more earnings in drivers' pockets, and reducing highway congestion.

01 · For Senders (Customers)

Pay only for what you send · Save 35%

Stop paying for an empty truck's return journey. Share empty return space and pay strictly for the goods weight you carry.

02 · For Truck Drivers

Fill your empty return bed · Extra monthly cash

Driving back empty is a complete waste of diesel and time. Fill your empty truck bed with paying goods with zero broker commission and instant UPI payment.

03 · For Roads & Nation

Fewer empty trucks · Less smoke & traffic

Every returning truck that carries goods prevents an unnecessary truck from hitting the highway. Saves diesel, cuts carbon smoke, and unclogs toll plazas.
